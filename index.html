What we collect

1) Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how budgetFR (“the App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the Android application. We are committed to a strict privacy-first, offline budgeting experience.

2) Information We Collect

We do not collect personal or financial information on our servers. budgetFR does not require account creation, login, phone number, email, or bank linking to function.

  • Stored locally on your device: Your display name, budgets, categories, expense logs, and app settings.
  • No Cloud Sync: Data is never transmitted to the internet. The app does not even request the Android INTERNET permission.

3) How We Use Information

Local data is used solely to make core budgeting features work:

  • Track your spending and calculate remaining balances.
  • Save your preferences and financial history.
  • Generate in-app analytics and charts.

We do not use your data for advertising profiles, tracking, or third-party analytics.

4) Permissions and Device Access

  • No Internet Permission: The app fundamentally cannot connect to the internet.
  • No Storage Permission: We do not require broad access to your device's photos or files. All data is kept securely within the app's isolated private sandbox.
We do not upload any financial data to our servers. Your finances are your business.

5) Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal or financial data with third parties for advertising or analytics. There are zero third-party SDKs (no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting) included in the app.

6) Data Retention and Deletion

Because all financial data is stored locally on your device, retention is entirely controlled by you.

  • Clearing the app data in Android settings will instantly permanently delete all your budgets and expenses.
  • Uninstalling the app completely removes all local app storage data associated with it.

7) Security

budgetFR utilizes Android's built-in application sandbox to protect your local data from other apps. Furthermore, we have explicitly disabled Android's cloud backup feature (`allowBackup="false"`) to ensure your financial data is never accidentally backed up to Google Drive in plain text.

8) Children’s Privacy

budgetFR is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from children or adults. The app does not require account signup and functions completely offline.

9) International Users

The app is operated by a developer based in India. As designed, personal data is never transmitted anywhere. Your local financial data remains securely on your physical device regardless of your geographical location.

10) Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page.
